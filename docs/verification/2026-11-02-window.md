## Onboarding

Built from the approved `plans/syrup/2026-11-02/onboarding-mockup.html`. A separate `org.syrup.SyrupUIVerify` host ran the real `OnboardingView` with temporary settings and secret storage, and drove it with click events sent through `NSWindow.sendEvent`.

- A fresh domain opened the window at launch at 540 x 460. The next launch resolved to closed.

- All steps were captured and inspected, including the empty and finished Taste states. The Taste result came from a seeded history record.
