## Republished ZIP

the maintainer reported that the published ZIP failed on install, with macOS saying it could not check the app for malware. The version stayed 0.8 build 8, and the app was not rebuilt.

**Root cause.** `ditto -c -k` stored each file's `com.apple.provenance` attribute as a `._` AppleDouble entry, 167 of the ZIP's 335 entries. `ditto` and Archive Utility merge those back into attributes. Other unzip tools write them as real files inside the signed bundle, including `_CodeSignature`, which breaks its seal. The earlier verification used only `ditto`, without quarantine, so it could not see this.

Evidence on this Mac and a second Mac, both macOS 27.0.1, with browser-style quarantine:

| Shipped ZIP unpacked with | `._` files | `spctl` |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Archive Utility | 0 | accepted, Notarized Developer ID |
| `ditto` | 0 | accepted, Notarized Developer ID |
| `unzip` | 167 | rejected, a sealed resource is missing or invalid |

The build itself was sound: `exported/Syrup.app` passed `codesign`, `spctl`, `stapler`, and `syspolicy_check`. Apple's log says Accepted, and Apple's ticket service has a record for its CDHash `<commit>`.

**Not established.** The exact dialog was not reproduced, and which tool or Mac produced the maintainer's failure is unknown. Archive Utility passed `spctl` here, and the app was not launched from a quarantined copy.

**Changes.**

- `script/release.py` zips with `--norsrc --noextattr --noqtn --noacl`. `verify_archive` rejects `._` and `__MACOSX` entries and checks the app after both `ditto` and `unzip`, at preparation and again at publish. It rejected the old ZIP.
- The same stapled app was rezipped. Its binary, ticket file, and CDHash are unchanged, so no new notarization was needed.
- `gh release upload --clobber` replaced the asset on `v0.8`. Public feed commit `<commit>` carries the new length and signature, because the signature covers the ZIP. Sparkle's key was used through `generate_appcast`; a first attempt with `sign_update` stopped at a Keychain prompt and was cancelled.
- The old ZIP, feed, and receipt stay in `dist/updates/0.8-8/` with `appledouble-broken` in their names.
